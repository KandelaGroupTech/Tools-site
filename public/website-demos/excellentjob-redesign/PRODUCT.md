## Platform

web

## Users
Primary: property managers and chief/senior building engineers at institutional property management firms in the DC, Northern Virginia and Maryland region (CBRE, Cushman & Wakefield, JLL, Carr, Lincoln, Cannon Hill and similar). They manage occupied commercial buildings and need a general contractor they can hand a tenant request, a spec suite, or a capital project to without worrying about it. They often arrive by referral and come to the site to confirm JOB is real, capable, and easy to reach, then call or send a request.

Secondary (confirmed by the site's contact form roles): building owners and asset managers, architects, and tenants.

## Product Purpose
Journey Office Builders LLC (JOB) is a tenant improvement general contractor based in Vienna, VA, founded 1989. The site exists to turn a property manager's visit into a call or a quote request, and to back up a referral with proof. Success: more inbound requests from property managers, and visitors leaving confident JOB will respond fast and deliver without surprises.

## Positioning
Responsiveness is the lead reason to pick JOB (confirmed by the user). JOB's stated standard of practice: acknowledge a request within the hour, quote within 24 to 48 hours, and deliver invoices that match the estimate with no surprises. Supported by: work built around active, tenant-occupied buildings rather than ground-up construction; preferred and approved vendor status in more than 40 buildings across leading institutional portfolios; a leadership team that includes a former 15-year CBRE Senior Chief Engineer, so JOB knows what property managers need from a GC.

## Operating Context
Visitors are busy professionals, often on a phone between building walk-throughs, or at a desk comparing vendors. The real workflow: property manager gets a tenant or owner request, calls or emails a trusted GC, GC walks the space, quotes, builds in an occupied building with minimal disruption, closes out. After-hours urgent tenant service line: 703-748-4600, press 1.
