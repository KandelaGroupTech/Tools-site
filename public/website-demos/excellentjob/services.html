How We Work

1

Preconstruction & Capital Planning

We guide owners through capital expenditure planning, budgeting, and project scoping early on, so your project starts with a realistic budget and timeline.

2

Permitting & Approvals

We manage the permitting process on your behalf, working directly with local jurisdictions to keep approvals moving and your project on schedule.

3

Construction Execution

Our core construction phase is where our teams execute on the plan, specializing in five distinct areas of commercial interior builds:

Tenant Improvements & Fit-Outs

We handle build-outs and renovations for both occupied and leasing-ready spaces, including white-box and spec suite construction, so your space is ready for tenants to move in and start working.

SCIF Construction

We build secure compartmented information facilities to ICD/ICS 705 standards, supporting your accreditation process from the ground up.

Building Renovations & Capital Improvements

From full interior overhauls to exterior upgrades and amenity space renovations, we help owners modernize their buildings and protect long-term asset value.

Demolition

We provide selective and full-scale demolition services, safely clearing space for your next renovation or build-out while protecting occupied areas around the work zone.

Flooring & Painting

Our team installs and replaces flooring, and handles interior and exterior painting and wall finishes, giving your space a fresh, finished look with minimal disruption.

4

Punch-List & Closeout

We provide punch-list and closeout services for projects built by other contractors, inspecting completed work and resolving outstanding items so your project reaches true completion.

5

Ongoing Tenant Services

Beyond construction, we provide ongoing maintenance and facilities support for occupied buildings, so your space stays in top condition long after move-in.
